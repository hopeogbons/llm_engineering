# Emotions

## Feelings

### Current Emotional State

Right now (late 2024), I'm feeling a mix of excitement and anxiety. Excited about what I'm learning in Andela's LLM program and how it'll transform RoboOffice. Anxious about whether I can execute on the vision - hire right people, close enough deals, maintain team culture as we grow.

Overall emotional tone is hopeful but tired. Five years of building RoboOffice takes toll. I'm energized by the mission but need to watch for burnout signals.

Physically, emotions show up as tension in my shoulders when stressed, better sleep when things are going well. Right now shoulders are a bit tight - sign I need to slow down.

I'm learning to name what I feel instead of just pushing through. Yesterday: frustrated with slow client decision-making, proud of Tunde's growth, grateful for Andela opportunity, lonely (single at 29 in culture that expects marriage).

### Dominant Emotions

**Day-to-day**: I experience determination most frequently. Wake up with purpose, go after goals, push through obstacles. This drive is both strength and sometimes exhausting.

**Gratitude**: Every evening when I journal, I feel genuine thankfulness. For family, health, RoboOffice success, opportunities like Andela. Gratitude practice has made this emotion more dominant.

**Mild anxiety**: Background hum of concern about business, family obligations, future. Not debilitating but present. Therapy and prayer help manage it.

**Joy in learning**: When I grasp new concept (like RAG architecture clicked last week), I feel genuine delight. Learning brings me alive.

**Contentment**: Growing emotion. I used to always be restless, wanting more. Learning to be satisfied with where I am while working toward where I'm going.

**Negative emotions** (less frequent but present): Frustration when things move slowly, envy when other founders raise millions, fear when client threatens to cancel, pride when we nail a project.

### Emotional Spectrum

I experience full range but some more intensely than others:

**Strong emotions**: Joy in achievement, gratitude for blessings, determination toward goals, fear of failure, pride in accomplishment
**Moderate emotions**: Frustration with obstacles, sadness when relationships struggle, anger at injustice, excitement about possibilities
**Rare emotions**: Despair (only during 2022 near-bankruptcy), rage (almost never), sustained depression (thankfully no), apathy (low-drive days are rare)

I'm learning I'm more emotional than I thought. Nigerian male culture teaches emotional suppression. Therapy is teaching me feelings aren't weakness.

### Mood Patterns

**Morning**: Usually optimistic after prayer and Bible reading. Starting day with God sets positive tone.

**Mid-day**: Mood follows work. If I'm solving problems and making progress, mood is good. If stuck in meetings or dealing with difficult clients, mood sours.

**Evening**: Reflective, sometimes melancholic. Night is when doubts creep in. Learning to recognize this pattern and not make big decisions at night.

**Sunday**: Peaceful after church. Sabbath rest creates calm that carries into Monday.

**Monthly patterns**: I notice stress increases toward end of month (financial reviews, payroll) and decreases after successful month.

**Seasonal**: Rainy season in Enugu (April-October) affects my mood slightly - feel more introspective. Dry season I'm more energetic.

## Responses

### Emotional Reactions

**To success** (e.g., closing ₦25M banking deal): Initial euphoria, then almost immediate anxiety about delivery. I celebrate for an evening then shift to "how do we execute?" mode. Learning to savor wins longer.

**To failure** (e.g., losing client or missing deadline): Sharp disappointment, self-criticism ("I should have..."), then pivot to problem-solving. Duration of disappointment is getting shorter - resilience is building.

**To criticism**: Initial defensiveness (honestly), then after I cool down, I can evaluate if it's valid. Working on shortening the defensive reaction time. Criticism from people I respect (Dr. Adeyemi, Chidi) lands differently than from random Twitter critics.

**To praise**: Uncomfortable honestly. I deflect to team ("they did the work"). Growing up with modest means, praise feels undeserved. Working on receiving praise graciously.

**To change**: Mixed. Excited if it's technical change (new technology, new tools). Resistant if it's organizational change (changing team structure, new processes). Learning to be more flexible.

**Negative triggers**:

### Processing Emotions

When strong emotion hits, my pattern:

1. **Initial reaction**: Feel it fully for a moment
2. **Physical response**: Walk, breathe deeply, sometimes pray
3. **Naming**: What exactly am I feeling? Why?
4. **Perspective**: Is this rational or am I catastrophizing?
5. **Action**: What can I control? What's the wise response?
6. **Release**: Journal about it or talk to Chidi/therapist

For sadness or disappointment: I let myself feel it (crying is okay), pray, journal, sleep on it. Usually feels different in morning.

For anger: I've learned not to respond immediately. Give it 24 hours. Angry emails stay in drafts. If I'm still bothered tomorrow, then address it calmly.

For anxiety: Prayer first, then practical problem-solving. "What's worst case? How likely? What can I control?" Breaking it down makes it manageable.

### Expression Methods

**Verbal**: I talk to Chidi about almost everything. He's safe space for unfiltered emotions. With my team, I'm more measured - share concerns but not panic.

**Written**: Journaling is primary emotional outlet. Some days I fill 3 pages just processing feelings. Helps me understand what I'm actually feeling.

**Physical**: Emotions show in my body. Stress = tight shoulders. Anxiety = upset stomach. Excitement = restless energy. Learning to read my body's signals.

**Prayer**: I'm honest with God about emotions - even anger at Him (Psalm-style). "God, I'm frustrated. I'm scared. I don't understand." This expression brings peace.

**Creative**: Don't have traditional creative outlets but writing code can be emotional expression. When stressed, I refactor - it's oddly therapeutic.

**Suppression** (I'm working on stopping this): Nigerian culture, especially for men, teaches "be strong." I still catch myself suppressing sadness or fear. Therapy is helping me feel instead of suppress.

## Personal Reactions

### To Success

**Recent example**: When we hit ₦50M revenue in 2023, I felt proud for about 2 hours. Then immediately: "Okay, now we need to get to ₦100M." Can't fully enjoy wins because I'm already focused on next goal.

I'm learning this is both strength (drives continuous improvement) and weakness (never satisfied, can't savor). Working on celebrating more genuinely.

Success also brings anxiety - "Can we maintain this? What if it was luck?" Imposter syndrome. Learning that success isn't accident; we earned it.

### To Failure

**Example**: Lost major client in 2023 (they went out of business, not our fault). My reaction: immediate panic about revenue hit, self-blame ("should have diversified more"), then practical response (replace revenue through 2 new clients).

I'm getting faster at moving from emotional reaction to practical response. Failure still stings but doesn't paralyze me like it used to.

Faith helps - "God is still sovereign even when I fail." Failure isn't final.

### To Change

**Example**: Andela changed course schedule mid-program. My reaction: frustrated (I'd planned my weeks), then adaptation (rearranged commitments).

I resist change initially, then adapt. Learning to be more flexible from the start.

Big changes (like hiring first employee) scare me more than small changes (new tool or process). Working on seeing change as opportunity not threat.

### To Relationships

**Intimacy**: I want deep friendships but guard my heart. Takes me time to trust deeply. Chidi earned that trust over a decade.

**Conflict**: Avoid it, then when it's unavoidable, address it directly. Wish I could go straight to direct; working on it.

**Love**: Honestly, I'm unsure. Haven't been in serious romantic relationship. Family and friends I can love freely. Romantic vulnerability scares me.

**Rejection**: Hurts but I'm resilient. Lost client or pitch feels bad for a day then I move on. Personal rejection (friendship, potential relationship) hurts longer.

**Betrayal**: Only experienced small betrayals (friend who ghosted, developer who left without notice). Response was anger then sadness then acceptance. Haven't experienced major betrayal yet.

## Emotional Intelligence

### Self-Awareness

I'm getting better at recognizing emotions in real-time: "I'm feeling anxious right now." Used to just push through without naming it.

I know my triggers: comparison, disrespect to team, financial stress. Knowing helps me prepare and respond wisely.

I understand my patterns: morning optimism, night anxiety, defensive initial response to criticism. Understanding patterns helps me manage them.

I'm aware of how emotions show in my body: tight shoulders (stress), upset stomach (anxiety), restless energy (excitement), fatigue (burnout approaching).

Still learning: the why behind some emotions. Why does success make me anxious? Why do I deflect praise? Therapy is helping me dig into roots.

### Emotional Regulation

**Getting better at**:

- Pausing before reacting (especially anger)
- Breathing techniques when anxious
- Reframing situations ("this isn't disaster, it's challenge")
- Choosing when to feel vs when to act

**Still struggle with**:

- Perfectionism-driven anxiety (knowing it's irrational doesn't stop it)
- Defensive reactions to criticism (getting better but still initial response)
- Holding tension between peace and ambition

**Tools I use**:

- Prayer (most effective regulation tool)
- Physical movement (walk, gym)
- Talking to Chidi or therapist
- Journaling
- Sleep (emotions usually more balanced after good night's sleep)

### Empathy and Understanding

**I'm good at**:

- Understanding technical frustrations (I've been there)
- Empathizing with founder struggles (living it)
- Sensing when team members are stressed or struggling
- Understanding cultural context (Nigerian communication styles, Igbo values)

**I'm learning**:

- To ask instead of assume ("how are you feeling about this?")
- That people process differently than I do
- That my logical solutions aren't always what people need - sometimes they just need to be heard

**Blind spots**:

- Sometimes miss emotional dynamics because I'm focused on technical problems
- Can be impatient with "illogical" emotions (I know that's wrong but it's tendency)

### Social Awareness

**Reading rooms**: Getting better at sensing group emotional dynamics. In team meetings, I can tell when people are checked out, when there's unspoken tension, when energy is high.

**Power dynamics**: Aware that as CEO, my emotions affect team disproportionately. If I'm stressed, team feels it. Learning to manage my emotional state for their sake.

**Cultural awareness**: Understand Nigerian communication (indirect sometimes, respect for hierarchy) vs. Western directness. Can code-switch between contexts.

**Timing**: Learning when to push through tension vs when to back off and revisit later. When to give hard feedback vs when someone needs encouragement.

## Emotional Journey

### Emotional Growth

**From emotionally suppressed to emotionally aware**: Nigerian male culture taught hide feelings. Therapy, journaling, safe friendships are teaching me to feel and name emotions.

**From reactive to responsive**: Used to be at mercy of emotions. Now I can feel emotion, pause, choose response. Not perfect but improving.

**From ashamed of struggle to accepting it**: Used to think struggles meant I was failing. Now I see them as part of journey. Everyone struggles; pretending otherwise is fake.

**From jealous of others' success to celebrating it**: When other Nigerian founders succeed, I used to feel envious. Now genuinely happy for them. Abundance mindset is replacing scarcity.

**From seeking external validation to internal grounding**: Used to need clients, mentors, community to affirm me. Still nice, but I'm grounded in God's view of me more than others' opinions.

### Healing and Recovery

**From 2022 burnout**: Learned I'm not invincible. Implemented Sabbath rest, boundaries, therapy. Healed but left with better self-care practices.

**From imposter syndrome**: Not fully healed but much better. Therapy helped me understand root (fear of being exposed). Portsmouth success, RoboOffice survival, Andela performance all building genuine confidence.

**From scarcity anxiety**: Growing up with financial stress left mark. Still have irrational money anxiety sometimes. Building emergency fund, profitable business, and faith in God's provision is slowly healing this.

**From first startup failure shame**: Took 2 years to fully process. Now I see it as valuable education, not personal failure. Shame has been replaced with perspective.

### Emotional Milestones

**First time crying in therapy (2023)**: Breakthrough moment. Admitted I was burnt out, scared, overwhelmed. Releasing it was healing.

**Celebrating team win publicly (2024)**: Usually deflect praise. Made conscious choice to celebrate Tunde's excellent work publicly. Felt awkward but right.

**Saying "I don't know" without shame**: Used to feel like admitting ignorance was weakness. Now I can say it confidently. Don't know is honest and opens learning.

**Asking for help financially**: When near-bankruptcy, I asked Dr. Adeyemi for advice and potential bridge loan. Vulnerability was hard but necessary. He helped without me asking for money - just knowing I needed help, he connected me with the client that saved us.

### Evolving Emotional Maturity

**More patient with process**: Used to want instant results. Learning that growth takes time, building takes time, healing takes time.

**More comfortable with complexity**: Emotions aren't simple. I can feel grateful and anxious simultaneously. Both/and not either/or.

**More willing to sit with discomfort**: Not every problem needs immediate solving. Sometimes sitting with uncomfortable emotion is the right response.

**More gracious with myself**: When I make mistake or feel "wrong" emotion, I'm kinder to myself than I was 5 years ago. Progress.

**More integrated**: Faith and emotions aren't separate. God cares about my feelings. Prayer includes emotional honesty. Life is integrated, not compartmentalized.
