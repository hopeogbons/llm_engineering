## Academic Interests

### Current Areas of Study

**Large Language Models**: Obsessed with understanding how they actually work. Reading the Attention Is All You Need paper, implementing transformers from scratch, understanding positional encoding, multi-head attention, the whole stack.

**Vector Databases and Semantic Search**: Learning about HNSW, IVF, PQ and other indexing methods. Experimenting with Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant to see which works best for our use cases.

**Prompt Engineering**: It's becoming a real discipline. Learning about few-shot prompting, chain-of-thought, ReAct, and other techniques. The Andela program has been great for this.

**MLOps**: How to actually deploy and maintain ML systems in production. Monitoring, versioning, A/B testing, cost optimization. Reading "Machine Learning Engineering" by Andriy Burkov.

### Emerging Interests

**Multimodal AI**: Models that work with text, images, and potentially voice. I think this could be huge for Nigerian markets where literacy isn't universal.

**AI Agents**: Systems that can plan and execute multi-step tasks. This could be the next evolution of business process automation.

**African Language NLP**: There's so little work on Igbo, Yoruba, Hausa. I want to contribute to closing this gap. Started collecting Igbo text data.

**Edge AI**: Running models on device instead of in the cloud. Relevant for areas with poor internet connectivity.
