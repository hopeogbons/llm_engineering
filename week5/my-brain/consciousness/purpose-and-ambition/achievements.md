# Achievements

## Personal Wins

### Academic Achievements

**First Class Honours from University of Portsmouth (2017)**: This was huge for me and my family. My parents who sold land to send me to university cried when I graduated. Final GPA: 3.87/4.0.

**Best Final Year Project in my cohort (2017)**: My process automation system beat 47 other projects. Professor Williams called it "industry-ready" which gave me confidence to eventually start RoboOffice.

**Consistently top 5% in the Andela LLM Engineering program**: Out of 83 participants globally, I'm ranking in the top performers. My Week 5 RAG implementation got highlighted as an exemplary solution.

**Self-taught Python and JavaScript** before university: Built my first functional website at 16, completely self-taught from a book I bought for ₦500 at Onitsha market.

### Personal Growth Milestones

**Started therapy in 2023**: Biggest personal growth decision I've made. Acknowledging I needed help with burnout and perfectionism was hard, but it's made me a better person and leader.

**Maintained consistent spiritual discipline** for 3 years: Morning prayer and Bible study, no matter how busy. There were days in 2022 when prayer was the only thing keeping me sane.

**Learned to delegate**: Forced myself to stop micromanaging in 2024. Watched my developers handle a complex client project without my direct involvement - terrifying but liberating.

**Built healthy boundaries with work**: In 2023, I instituted "no work on Sundays" rule for myself. Haven't broken it once. Sundays are for church, family, and rest.

### Overcoming Challenges

**Survived RoboOffice near-bankruptcy (2022)**: Had 3 months of runway left, couldn't make payroll, was personally broke. Prayed, hustled, landed a ₦18M deal 2 days before I would have had to shut down. Taught me resilience.

**Overcame imposter syndrome at Portsmouth**: Being in a foreign university, away from family, doubting if I belonged. Graduated top of my class. Proved to myself I could compete anywhere.

**Bounced back from failed first startup (2018)**: Lost ₦300,000 and 6 months on a marketplace app that went nowhere. Could have given up on entrepreneurship. Instead, learned and started RoboOffice with a better approach.

**Learned to say no to family financial requests**: Culturally difficult but necessary. Had to set boundaries about what I can and can't afford to give. Preserved my sanity and RoboOffice's finances.

### Character Development

**From reactive to thoughtful**: I used to snap at team members when they made mistakes. Now I pause, breathe, ask questions. My team notices and appreciates it.

**From prideful to humble**: Used to think I was the smartest person in the room. Portsmouth and starting a business humbled me quickly. Now I'm comfortable saying "I don't know" and asking for help.

**From secretive to transparent**: Used to hide when I was struggling. Now I'm open with my team about challenges and with friends about personal struggles. It's made relationships deeper.

**From scarcity to generosity**: Growing up with financial anxiety made me stingy. I've learned to be generous - with my time mentoring, with money to family and causes, with credit to my team.
