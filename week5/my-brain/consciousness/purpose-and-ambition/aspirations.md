# Aspirations

## Dreams

### Personal Dreams

I want to build a beautiful house in Enugu for my parents - maybe in Independence Layout or GRA. Something with a garden where my mom can grow her vegetables, and a study where my dad can read his newspapers in peace. Target: start building in 2027.

I dream of finding a partner who understands the startup life - someone who gets why I sometimes work late, who shares my faith, and who's building something meaningful themselves. Not rushing it, but I'm 29 now and my mom's pressure to get married is increasing monthly.

I want to travel more. I've been to the UK and a few West African countries, but I want to see Asia (especially Singapore and Japan for their tech scenes), and I want to take my parents on Hajj or a Holy Land tour - whichever they prefer.

I dream of having enough financial security that my family never worries about money again. No more stress about school fees for my siblings, medical bills for my parents, or whether the shop is making enough.

Learning to play the piano is a random personal goal. I've always loved music and I want that creative outlet that has nothing to do with code or business.

### Professional Dreams

My biggest professional dream is building a unicorn from Nigeria. Not "worth" a billion on paper, but genuinely generating massive value and serving millions of users across Africa. I think about this constantly.

I want RoboOffice to be the company people want to work for - not just for the salary but for the culture, growth opportunities, and the mission. When young developers in Nigeria list their dream companies, I want us on that list with Google and Andela.

Speaking at a major international conference like AWS re:Invent or Google I/O would be incredible. Representing Nigeria on that stage, sharing what we're building.

I dream of selling RoboOffice for enough money to solve my family's financial concerns for generations, then using the rest to fund other Nigerian founders. Becoming the investor I wish I had when I started.

Publishing a book about building tech companies in Africa - not the sanitized LinkedIn version but the real story with all the struggles, failures, and lessons. It would help the next generation.

### Creative Aspirations

I want to build more open-source tools for African developers. Imagine a whole suite of libraries that handle Nigerian-specific challenges - payment integrations, SMS, data validation, regulatory compliance. Make it easier for the next founder.

There's a documentary idea I've been thinking about - profiling successful tech entrepreneurs from unlikely places in Nigeria. Not Lagos or Abuja but smaller cities like Enugu, Ibadan, Calabar. Showing that you can build from anywhere.

I want to contribute to AI research for low-resource African languages. Work with universities to create better Igbo, Yoruba, and Hausa language models. That's important work that matters beyond just business.

Writing technical tutorials and guides is something I want to do more of. I've learned so much from free resources online; I want to create high-quality content that helps African developers level up.

### Lifestyle Goals

I want to own my time completely by 40. Wake up when I want, work on what I want, travel when I want. Building passive income streams and systems that don't need my daily involvement.

Health-wise, I want to maintain the fitness level of my 20s into my 40s and beyond. That means consistent exercise, better eating habits (less late-night coding snacks), and regular checkups.

Financial independence by 35 is the target. Not mega-rich, but enough invested that I could live comfortably without working if I chose to. Gives me freedom to take risks.

I want a lifestyle where I can be generous without thinking about it - help a cousin with school fees, donate to church, support causes I care about, invest in friends' businesses. Generosity should be normal, not sacrificial.

Having a workspace separate from home by 2026. I've been working from home since COVID and the boundaries are blurry. A proper office where I go to work and leave to come home.

## What I'm Striving Toward

### Current Focus Areas

**Mastering LLM engineering**: Right now, my main learning focus is getting genuinely good at building production LLM systems. The Andela program is just the start. I'm studying fine-tuning, RAG optimization, deployment strategies, cost management. By end of 2025, I should be an expert.

**Growing RoboOffice to ₦100M revenue**: We're at ₦50M, target is ₦100M by end of 2025. That means better sales processes, more aggressive marketing, expanding our service offerings with LLM capabilities.

**Building a sustainable team culture**: Investing heavily in team development - regular 1-on-1s, training budgets, clear career paths. I want people to stay and grow, not just pass through.

**Improving my leadership skills**: Working with my therapist and reading extensively about management. Doing a leadership course in Q1 2025. I can't keep winging this part.

**Maintaining work-life balance**: After the burnout of 2022-2023, I'm intentional about rest, boundaries, and Sundays off. Fighting my workaholic tendencies daily.

### Active Pursuits

**Fundraising preparation**: Working with Dr. Adeyemi to prepare for Series A fundraising in 2026. Building the pitch deck, getting financials in order, crafting the narrative.

**LLM product development**: Actively building our AI-powered document processing service. 3 clients in beta, aiming for general release in March 2025. This could be our breakthrough product.

**Public speaking**: Accepted to speak at TechCabal Battlefield in February 2025. Preparing the talk and using it to improve my speaking skills. Hopefully leads to more opportunities.

**Network building**: Deliberately expanding my professional network - joining founder communities, attending virtual conferences, being more active on LinkedIn and Twitter.

**Health improvement**: Started going to the gym 3x per week in November 2024. Also trying to cook more instead of always eating out. It's a struggle but I'm sticking with it.

### Development Goals

**Technical depth in AI/ML**: Want to go beyond using LLM APIs to understanding transformers at a fundamental level. Reading "Attention Is All You Need" paper, taking Andrei Karpathy's courses, implementing architectures from scratch.

**Business acumen**: Taking online courses in finance, fundraising, and growth strategy. I can code, but I need to get better at the business side of running a business.

**Communication skills**: Both written and verbal. Writing more blog posts to improve writing, joining a local Toastmasters group (virtually) to improve speaking.

**Product thinking**: Learning to think like a product manager, not just an engineer. Understanding user needs, prioritizing features, measuring impact. Reading "Inspired" by Marty Cagan.

**Sales and negotiation**: Practicing pitching, learning to handle objections, understanding enterprise sales cycles. We need to close bigger deals and I need to lead that.

### Experimentation and Exploration

**Testing different LLM providers**: Experimenting with OpenAI, Anthropic, Cohere, and local models. What works best for our use cases and budget?

**Trying new marketing channels**: Started a YouTube channel (3 videos so far, 127 subscribers). Testing if technical content can be a marketing funnel.

**Exploring partnerships**: In early conversations with AWS and Microsoft about their startup programs. Seeing if partnerships could help us reach more clients.

**Playing with voice AI**: Experimenting with building voice interfaces for our automation tools. Could be useful for clients with low literacy.

**Testing pricing models**: Moving some clients from project-based to subscription pricing. Seeing if it improves our revenue predictability.

## Future Self

At 40, I want to be known as a thoughtful, principled leader who built something meaningful while staying true to my values. Someone who made money without compromising integrity.

I want to be wiser - making better decisions faster, seeing around corners, understanding people and systems deeply. The kind of person younger founders come to for advice.

More patient and less reactive. I still get frustrated too easily when things don't go as planned. I want the emotional maturity to handle setbacks with grace.

Generous in every sense - with money, time, knowledge, credit. I've seen what scarcity mindset does to people and I want to be the opposite.

A better communicator - able to inspire teams, convince investors, explain complex technical concepts simply, and tell compelling stories.

Still learning and curious at 40, 50, 60. The day I think I know everything is the day I become irrelevant.
